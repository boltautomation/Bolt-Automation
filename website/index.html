Good to know

A few details.

Read the guide
What do I need to get started?+

Windows x64, Chrome or Brave, and Ollama with a downloaded local model. The package includes Node.js and PDF tools. Extract the whole folder, open Start Bolt.cmd, and follow the setup prompts. The default model download is about 2.5 GB.

Will every answer be correct?+

No. AI answers and source checks can be wrong. Bolt defaults to two source-based passes and guesses off, leaving unresolved questions for review. Check the original evidence before relying on an answer.

Where do my files go?+

The default local setup stores your PDF library and saved answers on your computer. Saved logins are encrypted for your Windows user. Online research and connected activities still visit external websites. This promotional website has no uploads, sign-up forms, or analytics scripts.

What am I allowed to automate?+

Only activities you're authorized to automate. Follow your school or organization's policies and the connected site's terms. Bolt is for educational use, not cheating, fabricated participation, reward manipulation, harm, or unauthorized access. Read the app usage terms.
