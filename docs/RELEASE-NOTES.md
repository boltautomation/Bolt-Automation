# V2 - Hemlock

Bolt now runs as a standalone desktop app with a slim, draggable title bar, centered version label, and minimize, maximize, and close controls. The glass sidebar keeps its lime colors, with a selection highlight that slides smoothly between tabs.

- Improved source recovery for embedded videos, transcripts, linked pages, PDF downloads, extensionless documents, and scanned documents. Offline image and PDF OCR is available on Windows.
- Archive notices and error pages are excluded from answer evidence. A confirmed alternative source recovers the unavailable public-health publication while retaining a real, reviewable citation.
- Optional visual-source recovery uses an already-installed local vision model only when needed. Machine interpretations and educated guesses remain labeled as unverified.
- Better research context, detailed review messages, saved settings, actual module scores, and persistent queue progress. Timing follows the selected settings without a fixed extra pause.

Download the package for Windows x64, Apple Silicon, or Intel Mac, extract the entire folder, and open the Bolt launcher. Chrome or Brave, Ollama, and a downloaded model are separate prerequisites. macOS 14 or newer is required; the Mac app is unsigned and not notarized.

Packages exclude saved accounts, sessions, PDFs, preferences, browser profiles, and activity history. Existing local data stays on your computer. Use Bolt only for educational work and activities you are authorized to automate, and review AI output against its sources. AI answers and guesses can be wrong.
